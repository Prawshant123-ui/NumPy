Skills covered: .loc, .iloc, filtering, adding, updating, deleting

Setup (for Parts 1 to 3, assume a fresh df: original 5 rows, 4 columns):

    import pandas as pd

    data = {
        'Ad_Budget': [2, 4, 6, 8, 10],
        'Discount': [5, 8, 10, 15, 20],
        'Customers': [120, 150, 190, 230, 260],
        'Sales': [35, 48, 62, 81, 97]
    }
    df = pd.DataFrame(data)
