NumPy Practice Questions (Essentials)

Data: a 2D array of restaurants with columns [id, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024]. The 26 questions below keep one question per skill and drop repeats.

Indexing and slicing
1. Get the first restaurant's complete record.
2. Get the sales of all restaurants in 2022.
3. Get the records of restaurants 1–5.
4. Get only the 2022 and 2023 sales columns.
5. Get the 2024 sales of restaurants 3, 5 and 7.

Calculations
6. Find the average sales in 2021.
7. Find the highest sales in 2024.
8. Find the average sales of each restaurant across all 4 years.
9. Find the highest sales of each restaurant.
10. Find the total sales across all restaurants for each year.

Sorting
11. Sort the restaurants by their 2024 sales.
12. Find the top 3 restaurants by 2024 sales.
13. Use np.argsort() to get the ranking of restaurants by 2024 sales.

Filtering with masks
14. Find restaurants whose 2024 sales are greater than 250000.
15. Find restaurants whose 2024 AND 2023 sales are both greater than 200000.
16. Find restaurants whose 2024 sales are greater than 250000 OR 2021 sales are greater than 200000.
17. Get the restaurant IDs where 2024 sales are greater than 250000.

np.where()
18. Label restaurants "High" if 2024 sales ≥ 250000, otherwise "Low".
19. Find the indexes of restaurants whose sales increased every year.
20. Find restaurants where 2024 sales are at least 50000 higher than 2021 sales.

Fancy indexing
21. Select the 2024 sales of the restaurants at row indexes 1, 4 and 7.
22. Select these individual values: restaurant 1 → 2021 sales, restaurant 4 → 2023 sales, restaurant 7 → 2024 sales.

Array manipulation
23. Add a new restaurant: np.array([11, 145000, 170000, 195000, 225000]) (hint: it is 1D, so reshape it first).
24. Remove the restaurant with ID 6.
25. Add a ratings column with hstack(): np.array([[4.2],[4.5],[4.7],[4.3],[4.6],[4.1],[4.8],[4.0],[4.4],[4.5]]).

Challenge
26. Build an array of [restaurant_id, average_sales], then sort it from highest to lowest average sales.
